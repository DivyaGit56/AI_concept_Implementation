DEMOBANK - HOME LOAN POLICY

Section: Overview

DemoBank Home Loans finance purchase, construction, renovation, and balance transfer of residential properties.

Section: Eligibility Criteria

- Age: 21 to 65 years at loan maturity
- Minimum annual income: ₹3,00,000 (individual) / ₹5,00,000 (joint application)
- Minimum employment/business tenure: 2 years
- CIBIL score: Minimum 750
- Property must be within approved locations list

Section: Loan Amount and Terms

- Minimum loan: ₹5,00,000
- Maximum loan: ₹5,00,00,000
- LTV (Loan-to-Value): Up to 90% for loans up to ₹30 lakh, 80% for loans above ₹30 lakh
- Tenure: Up to 30 years
- Interest rate (floating): 8.50% to 9.75% per annum
- Interest rate (fixed): 9.25% to 10.50% per annum (first 5 years)
- Processing fee: 0.50% of loan amount + GST

Section: Required Documents

Applicant Documents:
- PAN Card and Aadhaar Card
- Last 6 months salary slips (salaried)
- Last 2 years ITR (self-employed)
- Last 12 months bank statements
- Employment/business proof

Property Documents:
- Sale agreement / allotment letter
- Approved building plan
- Occupancy certificate (for ready properties)
- Property tax receipts
- Encumbrance certificate (last 13 years)
- NOC from builder/society

Section: Required Documents for Home Loan (Summary)

For salaried applicants:
1. Identity proof (PAN + Aadhaar)
2. Address proof
3. Last 6 months salary slips
4. Form 16 for last 2 years
5. Last 12 months bank statements
6. Property documents as listed above

For self-employed applicants:
1. Identity and address proof
2. Last 3 years ITR with computation
3. Last 12 months bank statements (business and personal)
4. Business registration proof
5. Property documents as listed above

Section: Application Process

Step 1: Submit home loan application (HL-001 form)
Step 2: Document verification and property legal check (5-7 days)
Step 3: Property valuation by approved valuer
Step 4: Credit appraisal and sanction (7-10 business days)
Step 5: Sign loan agreement and register mortgage
Step 6: Disbursement as per construction/purchase schedule

Section: Tax Benefits

- Section 80C: Deduction up to ₹1,50,000 on principal repayment
- Section 24(b): Deduction up to ₹2,00,000 on interest payment (self-occupied)
- Additional ₹1,50,000 under Section 80EEA for first-time homebuyers (conditions apply)

Section: Prepayment

- Floating rate loans: No prepayment penalty
- Fixed rate loans: 2% penalty if prepaid within fixed rate period
- Partial prepayment allowed once every 6 months (minimum ₹50,000)
