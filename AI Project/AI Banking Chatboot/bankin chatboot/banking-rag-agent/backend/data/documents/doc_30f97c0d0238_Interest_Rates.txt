DEMOBANK - INTEREST RATES (Effective September 2026)

Section: Savings Account Interest Rates

Regular Savings Account: 3.50% per annum (calculated on daily balance, credited quarterly)
Senior Citizen Savings Account: 4.00% per annum
Salary Account: 3.50% per annum
Basic Savings (BSBD): 3.50% per annum

Section: Fixed Deposit (FD) Interest Rates

Tenure 7-45 days: 3.00% p.a.
Tenure 46-90 days: 4.00% p.a.
Tenure 91-180 days: 4.50% p.a.
Tenure 181-364 days: 5.50% p.a.
Tenure 1 year: 6.75% p.a.
Tenure 2 years: 7.00% p.a.
Tenure 3 years: 7.10% p.a.
Tenure 5 years: 7.25% p.a.

Senior Citizen Additional Rate: +0.50% on all FD tenures

Section: Recurring Deposit (RD) Rates

Tenure 6 months to 10 years: Same as FD rates for corresponding tenure
Minimum monthly installment: ₹500
Maximum: No limit

Section: Loan Interest Rates

Personal Loan: 10.50% to 18.00% p.a. (based on credit score)
Home Loan (Floating): 8.50% to 9.75% p.a.
Home Loan (Fixed - 5 years): 9.25% to 10.50% p.a.
Education Loan: 9.00% to 12.50% p.a.
Vehicle Loan: 8.75% to 11.50% p.a.
Business Loan: 12.00% to 18.00% p.a.

Section: Overdraft Interest Rates

Overdraft against FD: FD rate + 1.00%
Overdraft against property: 10.50% to 12.00% p.a.
Clean overdraft (pre-approved): 14.00% to 16.00% p.a.

Section: Important Notes

- Interest rates are subject to change based on RBI policy and bank discretion
- Rates applicable at time of booking apply for entire FD tenure
- Premature FD withdrawal: 1% penalty on applicable rate
- TDS applicable if interest exceeds ₹40,000 per year (₹50,000 for senior citizens)

Section: MCLR (Marginal Cost of Funds Based Lending Rate)

Current MCLR (September 2026):
- Overnight: 8.10%
- 1 Month: 8.15%
- 3 Months: 8.25%
- 6 Months: 8.35%
- 1 Year: 8.50%

All floating rate loans are linked to 1-Year MCLR + spread.
