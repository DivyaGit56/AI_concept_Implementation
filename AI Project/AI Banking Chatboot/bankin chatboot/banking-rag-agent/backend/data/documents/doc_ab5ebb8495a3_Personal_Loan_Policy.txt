DEMOBANK - PERSONAL LOAN POLICY

Section: Overview

DemoBank Personal Loans provide unsecured financing for personal needs including medical expenses, travel, wedding, home renovation, and debt consolidation.

Section: Eligibility Criteria

- Age: 21 to 60 years at loan maturity
- Minimum monthly income: ₹25,000 (salaried) / ₹3,00,000 annual (self-employed)
- Minimum employment tenure: 2 years (salaried) / 3 years in business (self-employed)
- CIBIL score: Minimum 700
- Existing DemoBank customer preferred (not mandatory)

Section: Loan Amount and Tenure

- Minimum loan amount: ₹50,000
- Maximum loan amount: ₹25,00,000
- Tenure: 12 to 60 months
- Interest rate: 10.50% to 18.00% per annum (based on credit profile)
- Processing fee: 1% of loan amount + GST (minimum ₹2,500)

Section: Required Documents

Identity and Address Proof:
- PAN Card (mandatory)
- Aadhaar Card
- Address proof (utility bill/bank statement)

Income Proof (Salaried):
- Last 3 months salary slips
- Last 6 months bank statements
- Form 16 or ITR for last 2 years
- Employment certificate

Income Proof (Self-Employed):
- Last 2 years ITR with computation
- Last 12 months bank statements (business account)
- GST registration / business proof
- Profit & Loss statement and Balance Sheet

Additional Documents:
- Loan application form (PL-001)
- Passport-size photographs (2)
- Existing loan statement (if any)

Section: Application Process

Step 1: Apply online at demobank.com/personal-loan or visit branch
Step 2: Submit application with documents
Step 3: Credit assessment (2-3 business days)
Step 4: Loan sanction letter issued
Step 5: Sign loan agreement
Step 6: Disbursement within 24-48 hours of agreement

Section: Repayment

- EMI auto-debit from DemoBank account on fixed date
- Prepayment allowed after 12 EMIs with 2% charge on outstanding
- Late payment penalty: 2% per month on overdue amount
- No prepayment penalty for full closure after 50% tenure completion

Section: Foreclosure

Full foreclosure permitted after 12 EMIs:
- Foreclosure charge: 2% of outstanding principal
- No charge after 50% of tenure completed
- NOC issued within 7 business days of full payment
