DEMOBANK - DEBIT CARD POLICY

Section: Overview

All DemoBank savings and current account holders receive a complimentary debit card linked to their account.

Section: Card Types

- Classic Debit Card: Standard ATM card with ₹25,000 daily limit
- Platinum Debit Card: Premium card with ₹1,00,000 daily limit and lounge access
- Business Debit Card: For current accounts, ₹2,00,000 daily limit

Section: Daily Transaction Limits

Classic Debit Card:
- ATM withdrawal: ₹25,000 per day
- POS/E-commerce: ₹50,000 per day
- Contactless: ₹5,000 per transaction (₹25,000 daily)

Platinum Debit Card:
- ATM withdrawal: ₹50,000 per day
- POS/E-commerce: ₹1,00,000 per day
- International transactions: Enabled on request

Section: PIN Generation and Security

- Green PIN: Generate PIN at any DemoBank ATM using OTP sent to registered mobile
- Never share PIN, CVV, or OTP with anyone
- Enable transaction alerts via SMS and email
- Set daily limits via mobile banking app

Section: Card Not Working - Troubleshooting

If your debit card is not working, try these steps:

Step 1: Verify card is activated (new cards require activation via ATM or mobile app)
Step 2: Check if card is blocked (view status in mobile banking > Cards)
Step 3: Verify sufficient account balance
Step 4: Check if daily transaction limit exceeded (reset via app)
Step 5: For online transactions, ensure international/domestic e-commerce is enabled
Step 6: Clean chip/contactless area and try again at POS terminal
Step 7: Try a different ATM or POS terminal
Step 8: If issue persists, call 1800-123-4567 or visit branch

Common Issues:
- "Insufficient funds": Check account balance
- "Daily limit exceeded": Modify limits in mobile app or wait until next day
- "Card blocked": Unblock via app or call helpline
- "Invalid PIN": Reset PIN at DemoBank ATM using Green PIN
- "Transaction declined - contact bank": Card may be blocked due to suspicious activity

Section: Card Replacement

- Damaged card: Free replacement, 7-10 business days
- Lost/stolen: Block immediately, replacement fee ₹200
- Expired card: Auto-renewed and dispatched 30 days before expiry

Section: International Usage

Enable international usage via:
- Mobile app: Cards > International Usage > Enable
- Net banking: Cards > Manage > International Transactions
- Call helpline: 1800-123-4567
Foreign transaction markup: 3.5% on all international transactions
