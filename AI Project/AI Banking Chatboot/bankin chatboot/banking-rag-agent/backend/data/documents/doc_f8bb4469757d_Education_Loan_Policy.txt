DEMOBANK - EDUCATION LOAN POLICY

Section: Overview

DemoBank Education Loans support higher education in India and abroad for undergraduate, postgraduate, and professional courses.

Section: Eligibility Criteria

- Student age: 16 to 35 years
- Admission confirmed to recognized institution (AICTE/UGC/foreign accredited)
- Co-applicant (parent/guardian) mandatory with stable income
- CIBIL score of co-applicant: Minimum 650

Section: Loan Amount and Coverage

Domestic Education:
- Up to ₹10,00,000 without collateral
- Up to ₹20,00,000 with collateral
- Covers tuition, hostel, exam fees, books, laptop

Foreign Education:
- Up to ₹20,00,000 without collateral
- Up to ₹1,50,00,000 with collateral
- Covers tuition, living expenses, travel, health insurance

Interest rate: 9.00% to 12.50% per annum
Moratorium period: Course duration + 6 months
Repayment tenure: Up to 15 years after moratorium

Section: Required Documents

Student Documents:
- Admission letter from institution
- Academic records (10th, 12th, graduation marksheets)
- PAN Card and Aadhaar Card
- Passport and visa (for foreign education)
- Course fee structure from institution

Co-applicant Documents:
- Identity and address proof
- Income proof (salary slips/ITR for 2 years)
- Last 12 months bank statements
- Collateral documents (if loan exceeds threshold)

Section: Application Process

Step 1: Apply with admission letter at branch or demobank.com/education-loan
Step 2: Document verification (3-5 business days)
Step 3: Loan sanction letter
Step 4: Disbursement directly to institution (tuition) and student account (other expenses)

Section: Repayment and Subsidy

- Repayment starts after moratorium period
- Interest subsidy under Central Sector Interest Subsidy (CSIS) for economically weaker sections
- No prepayment penalty
- Tax benefit under Section 80E for entire interest paid (no upper limit)

Section: Collateral Requirements

Loans above ₹7.5 lakh (domestic) or ₹15 lakh (foreign) require:
- Property mortgage, OR
- Fixed deposit lien, OR
- Third-party guarantee from DemoBank account holder
