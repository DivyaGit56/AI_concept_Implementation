DEMOBANK - CREDIT CARD POLICY

Section: Card Variants

DemoBank offers the following credit cards:
- Classic Card: Entry-level, annual fee ₹500 (waived on ₹50,000 annual spend)
- Gold Card: Mid-tier, annual fee ₹1,500 (waived on ₹1,00,000 annual spend)
- Platinum Card: Premium, annual fee ₹5,000 (waived on ₹3,00,000 annual spend)
- Corporate Card: For business expenses, annual fee ₹2,500

Section: Eligibility

- Age: 21 to 65 years
- Minimum annual income: ₹3,00,000 (Classic), ₹5,00,000 (Gold), ₹10,00,000 (Platinum)
- CIBIL score: Minimum 750
- Existing DemoBank relationship preferred

Section: Credit Limits

- Classic: ₹25,000 to ₹1,00,000
- Gold: ₹1,00,000 to ₹3,00,000
- Platinum: ₹3,00,000 to ₹10,00,000
- Limits assigned based on income, credit score, and existing obligations

Section: Interest and Charges

- Interest rate on revolving credit: 3.49% per month (42% per annum)
- Cash advance fee: 2.5% of amount (minimum ₹500)
- Late payment fee: ₹750 to ₹1,200 based on outstanding
- Over-limit fee: ₹500
- Foreign transaction markup: 3.5%

Section: Billing and Payment

- Billing cycle: Monthly, statement generated on 1st of each month
- Payment due date: 18 days from statement date
- Minimum payment: 5% of total outstanding
- Payment via auto-debit, net banking, UPI, or cheque

Section: Rewards Program

- Classic: 1 reward point per ₹100 spent
- Gold: 2 reward points per ₹100 spent
- Platinum: 3 reward points per ₹100 spent
- 1 reward point = ₹0.25 redemption value
- Bonus points on dining, fuel, and online shopping categories

Section: Lost or Stolen Card

Immediately block card via:
- Mobile app: Cards > Block Card
- Helpline: 1800-123-4567 (24/7)
- SMS: BLOCK <last 4 digits> to 5676766
Replacement card issued within 5-7 business days at no charge for first replacement per year

Section: Dispute Resolution

Report unauthorized transactions within 30 days of statement date.
Provisional credit provided within 7 business days pending investigation.
Full investigation completed within 45 days.
