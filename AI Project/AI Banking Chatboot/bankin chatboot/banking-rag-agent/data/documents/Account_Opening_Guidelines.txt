DEMOBANK - ACCOUNT OPENING GUIDELINES

Section: Eligibility Criteria

To open a savings account with DemoBank, applicants must:
- Be an Indian resident aged 18 years or above
- For minor accounts, a parent or legal guardian must operate the account
- Non-resident Indians (NRIs) may open NRE/NRO accounts with additional documentation

Section: Required Documents

Primary Identity Proof (any one):
- Aadhaar Card
- PAN Card
- Passport
- Voter ID Card
- Driving License

Address Proof (any one):
- Aadhaar Card
- Utility bill (not older than 3 months)
- Bank statement (not older than 3 months)
- Rental agreement (registered)

Additional Requirements:
- Two recent passport-size photographs
- PAN Card is mandatory for all account types
- Initial deposit as per account type (minimum ₹1,000 for regular savings)

Section: Account Types and Minimum Balance

Regular Savings Account:
- Minimum average monthly balance: ₹5,000
- Initial deposit: ₹1,000
- Free debit card included

Basic Savings Account (BSBD):
- Zero minimum balance
- Maximum 4 free withdrawals per month
- No cheque book facility

Salary Account:
- Zero minimum balance while salary is credited
- Requires employer tie-up with DemoBank
- Premium debit card with higher ATM limits

Senior Citizen Account:
- Minimum balance: ₹2,500
- Additional 0.50% interest on savings
- Priority service at branches

Section: Account Opening Process

Step 1: Visit nearest DemoBank branch or apply online at demobank.com/open-account
Step 2: Fill account opening form (Form AOF-001)
Step 3: Submit KYC documents (original + photocopy)
Step 4: Complete in-person verification (IPV) if applying online
Step 5: Make initial deposit
Step 6: Account activated within 24-48 hours
Step 7: Debit card dispatched within 7-10 business days

Section: Online Account Opening

DemoBank offers instant digital account opening via Video KYC:
- Complete application at demobank.com/instant-account
- Upload Aadhaar and PAN
- Complete Video KYC with bank officer
- Account activated within 2 hours
- Virtual debit card issued immediately
