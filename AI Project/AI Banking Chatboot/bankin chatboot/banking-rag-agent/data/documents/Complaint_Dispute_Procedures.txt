DEMOBANK - COMPLAINT AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION PROCEDURES

Section: Overview

DemoBank is committed to resolving customer complaints fairly and promptly in accordance with RBI Banking Ombudsman Scheme 2021.

Section: Complaint Channels

Level 1 - Branch/Customer Care:
- Visit home branch and submit written complaint
- Call 24/7 helpline: 1800-123-4567
- Email: support@demobank.com
- Mobile app: Help > Raise Complaint
- Website: demobank.com/complaints

Level 2 - Grievance Redressal Officer (GRO):
- Email: gro@demobank.com
- Write to: Grievance Redressal Officer, DemoBank HQ, Mumbai - 400001
- Response within 7 working days

Level 3 - Principal Nodal Officer:
- Email: pno@demobank.com
- Response within 7 working days of GRO escalation

Level 4 - Banking Ombudsman:
- If not satisfied with bank's resolution within 30 days
- File complaint at https://cms.rbi.org.in
- Free of charge, decision binding on bank up to ₹20 lakh

Section: Complaint Categories and Resolution Timelines

Account-related issues: 7 working days
ATM/Card disputes: 7 working days (provisional credit within 3 days)
Fund transfer failures: 24-48 hours
Loan processing delays: 15 working days
Unauthorized transactions: 7 working days (provisional credit within 7 days)
Service quality: 7 working days

Section: How to File a Complaint

Step 1: Gather details (account number, transaction reference, date, description)
Step 2: Submit complaint through preferred channel
Step 3: Receive complaint reference number (format: CMP-XXXXX)
Step 4: Track status via app/website using reference number
Step 5: Receive resolution within stipulated timeline
Step 6: Escalate to next level if unsatisfied

Section: Dispute Resolution for Unauthorized Transactions

Report within 3 days of notification: Zero liability
Report within 4-7 days: Maximum liability ₹10,000
Report after 7 days: Liability as per bank investigation
Customer negligence (shared PIN/OTP): Full liability

Required information for dispute:
- Transaction date and amount
- Transaction reference number
- Whether card/credentials were shared
- Police complaint (for amounts above ₹25,000)

Section: Complaint Tracking

Track complaint status:
- Mobile app: Help > My Complaints
- Website: demobank.com/track-complaint
- SMS updates at each stage
- Email notification on resolution

Section: Compensation Policy

For service deficiency causing financial loss:
- Interest at savings rate for delayed credit
- Refund of erroneous charges with interest
- Compensation up to ₹1,000 for documented mental agony (Ombudsman cases)

Section: Feedback

After complaint resolution, customers receive feedback survey.
Persistent issues may be escalated to Customer Service Committee.

Section: Support Ticket Creation

For unresolved issues via AI chat support:
- Support ticket automatically created with ID (format: TKT-XXXXX)
- Assigned to customer service team within 24 hours
- Updates sent via SMS and email
- Target resolution: 3-5 business days
