DEMOBANK - KYC (KNOW YOUR CUSTOMER) POLICY

Section: Overview

DemoBank complies with RBI Master Direction on KYC dated February 25, 2016 (updated). All customers must complete KYC verification before availing full banking services.

Section: KYC Documents Required

For Individual Customers:
1. Proof of Identity: PAN Card (mandatory) + any one of Aadhaar/Passport/Voter ID/Driving License
2. Proof of Address: Aadhaar/Utility Bill/Rental Agreement/Bank Statement (not older than 3 months)
3. Recent passport-size photograph
4. Form 60 (if PAN not available, with valid reason)

For Non-Individual Entities:
- Certificate of Incorporation
- Memorandum and Articles of Association
- Board Resolution for account opening
- PAN of entity
- KYC of authorized signatories
- GST Registration (if applicable)

Section: KYC Update Frequency

- Low Risk customers: Every 10 years
- Medium Risk customers: Every 8 years
- High Risk customers: Every 2 years
- Re-KYC must be completed within 30 days of notification

Section: Video KYC Process

DemoBank offers Video KYC for remote onboarding:
1. Customer initiates Video KYC via mobile app or website
2. Live interaction with authorized bank officer (5-10 minutes)
3. Officer verifies identity documents on camera
4. Geo-tagging and liveness detection performed
5. KYC completed and account activated within 2 hours

Section: CKYC (Central KYC)

DemoBank participates in Central KYC Registry (CKYCR):
- 14-digit KYC Identification Number (KIN) issued upon registration
- KYC data shared across financial institutions with customer consent
- Reduces duplicate KYC submissions

Section: Consequences of Non-Compliance

Accounts with incomplete or expired KYC will be:
- Restricted from debit transactions after 30-day notice
- Blocked from credit transactions after 60-day notice
- Account frozen if KYC not updated within 90 days of notice

Section: Document Verification Standards

All documents must be:
- Original documents presented for verification
- Self-attested photocopies accepted for branch applications
- E-documents via DigiLocker accepted for online applications
- Documents in languages other than English/Hindi require certified translation
