DEMOBANK - FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

Section: General Banking Information

Q: What are DemoBank's operating hours?
A: DemoBank branches operate Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and Saturday 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM. Internet and mobile banking are available 24/7.

Q: How can I contact customer support?
A: Call our 24/7 helpline at 1800-123-4567, email support@demobank.com, or use the in-app chat support feature.

Q: Is my money safe with DemoBank?
A: Yes. DemoBank is regulated by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) and deposits are insured up to ₹5 lakh per depositor under DICGC guidelines.

Section: Account Services

Q: What types of accounts does DemoBank offer?
A: DemoBank offers Savings Accounts, Current Accounts, Salary Accounts, Senior Citizen Accounts, and Minor Accounts.

Q: What is the minimum balance for a savings account?
A: Regular savings accounts require a minimum average monthly balance of ₹5,000. Basic savings accounts have zero minimum balance requirements.

Q: How do I close my account?
A: Visit your home branch with valid ID proof, submit an account closure form, settle all dues, and surrender unused cheque books and debit cards.

Section: Digital Banking

Q: How do I register for internet banking?
A: Visit demobank.com/register, enter your account number and registered mobile number, verify OTP, and set your login credentials.

Q: What if I forget my internet banking password?
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page, verify your identity via OTP sent to your registered mobile, and create a new password.

Q: Is mobile banking secure?
A: Yes. DemoBank mobile banking uses 256-bit SSL encryption, biometric authentication, and transaction OTP verification.

Section: Cards

Q: How long does it take to receive a new debit card?
A: Debit cards are typically delivered within 7-10 business days after account opening or card request.

Q: What should I do if my card is lost or stolen?
A: Immediately block your card via mobile banking, internet banking, or by calling 1800-123-4567. Request a replacement card through your branch or online.

Section: Loans

Q: What types of loans does DemoBank offer?
A: DemoBank offers Personal Loans, Home Loans, Education Loans, Vehicle Loans, and Business Loans.

Q: Can I prepay my loan?
A: Yes. Personal loans can be prepaid after 12 EMIs with a 2% prepayment charge. Home loans have no prepayment penalty for floating rate loans.
