DEMOBANK - BANKING CHARGES AND FEES

Section: Account Maintenance Charges

Regular Savings Account:
- Non-maintenance of minimum balance (₹5,000): ₹500 per quarter
- Account dormancy (no transaction for 24 months): ₹200 per year

Current Account:
- Minimum average balance: ₹25,000
- Non-maintenance penalty: ₹1,500 per quarter

Section: Transaction Charges

Cash Deposit:
- Home branch: Free (up to ₹2 lakh per day)
- Non-home branch: ₹5 per ₹1,000 (minimum ₹50)

Cash Withdrawal:
- Home branch: Free (up to ₹50,000 per day)
- Non-home branch: ₹5 per ₹1,000 (minimum ₹50)

Cheque Book:
- First 25 leaves per year: Free
- Additional cheque book: ₹3 per leaf

Section: Digital Transaction Charges

NEFT (via branch):
- Up to ₹10,000: ₹2.50 + GST
- ₹10,001 to ₹1 lakh: ₹5 + GST
- Above ₹1 lakh: ₹25 + GST
- NEFT via internet/mobile banking: Free

RTGS (via branch):
- ₹25 + GST (any amount)
- RTGS via internet/mobile banking: Free

IMPS:
- Up to ₹1,000: Free
- ₹1,001 to ₹1 lakh: ₹5 + GST
- Above ₹1 lakh: ₹15 + GST

UPI: Free for all transactions

Section: Card Charges

Debit Card:
- Annual fee (Classic): Free
- Annual fee (Platinum): ₹500 (waived on ₹50,000 annual spend)
- Replacement (lost/stolen): ₹200
- PIN regeneration at non-DemoBank ATM: ₹25

Credit Card:
- Classic annual fee: ₹500
- Gold annual fee: ₹1,500
- Platinum annual fee: ₹5,000
- Cash advance: 2.5% (minimum ₹500)
- Late payment: ₹750 to ₹1,200

Section: Loan Processing Fees

Personal Loan: 1% of loan amount + GST (minimum ₹2,500)
Home Loan: 0.50% of loan amount + GST
Education Loan: 0.50% of loan amount + GST (maximum ₹10,000)
Vehicle Loan: 1% of loan amount + GST

Section: Other Charges

Demand Draft: ₹5 per ₹1,000 (minimum ₹50, maximum ₹15,000)
Stop payment (cheque): ₹100 per instruction
Account statement (branch): ₹100 per statement
SMS alerts: ₹15 per quarter
Physical passbook update: Free at branch

Section: GST

All charges are exclusive of GST at 18% unless stated otherwise.
