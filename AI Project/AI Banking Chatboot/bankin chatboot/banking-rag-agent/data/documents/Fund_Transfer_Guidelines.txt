DEMOBANK - FUND TRANSFER GUIDELINES

Section: NEFT (National Electronic Funds Transfer)

Availability: 24x7 including holidays (since December 2019)
Minimum amount: No minimum
Maximum amount: No maximum
Settlement: Hourly batches, 24/7
Charges (branch): ₹2.50 to ₹25 based on amount
Charges (digital): Free

How to transfer via NEFT:
1. Add beneficiary in internet/mobile banking
2. Wait 30 minutes for beneficiary activation (cooling period)
3. Initiate NEFT transfer with beneficiary details
4. Confirm with OTP
5. Reference number generated for tracking

Section: RTGS (Real Time Gross Settlement)

Availability: 24x7 including holidays
Minimum amount: ₹2,00,000
Maximum amount: No maximum
Settlement: Real-time
Charges (branch): ₹25 + GST
Charges (digital): Free

Section: IMPS (Immediate Payment Service)

Availability: 24x7
Minimum amount: ₹1
Maximum amount: ₹5,00,000 per transaction
Settlement: Instant
Charges: ₹5 to ₹15 based on amount

IMPS using MMID:
- Mobile number + 7-digit MMID required
- Generate MMID via mobile banking app

Section: UPI (Unified Payments Interface)

Availability: 24x7
Per transaction limit: ₹1,00,000 (may vary by bank)
Daily limit: ₹1,00,000
Charges: Free
Supported apps: DemoBank UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm

Section: Internal Transfer

Transfer between DemoBank accounts:
- Free via all channels
- Instant settlement
- No beneficiary cooling period for own accounts

Section: International Wire Transfer

SWIFT transfers supported for outward remittance:
- Processing time: 2-5 business days
- Charges: ₹500 to ₹1,000 + correspondent bank charges
- FEMA compliance required
- Purpose code mandatory (LRS limit: USD 2,50,000 per year)

Section: Transfer Limits (Digital Banking)

Default daily limits:
- NEFT/RTGS/IMPS: ₹10,00,000
- UPI: ₹1,00,000
- Increase limits: Visit branch with request form and ID proof

Section: Failed Transfer Resolution

If transfer fails but amount debited:
- Amount auto-reversed within 24 hours for NEFT/RTGS
- IMPS reversals within 1 hour
- If not reversed, raise dispute via app or call 1800-123-4567
- Provide transaction reference number and date

Section: Beneficiary Management

- Maximum 50 beneficiaries per account
- Cooling period: 30 minutes for new beneficiaries (NEFT/RTGS)
- No cooling period for IMPS up to ₹5,000
- Delete beneficiary anytime via digital banking
